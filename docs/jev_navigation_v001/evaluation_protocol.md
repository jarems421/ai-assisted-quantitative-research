# Jev Navigation V001: smallest legitimate usefulness evaluation

**PROPOSED — not registered, no model call or human judgments authorised by this file.** The public synthetic fixtures test implementation and diagnostics only. They cannot establish real Jev usefulness, confidence calibration or readiness for deployment. Do not reuse the retired five-question critic or its human-versus-Jev labels.

## First freeze the task, inputs and labels

Use already retained, authorised material only. Propose six small research questions: two evidence prospecting, two memory and two workflow cases, including ambiguous and adverse cases. For evidence, use about 24 candidate passages in total, with several original measurement leads, keyword decoys, an outcome reconstructed from its predictor, an ambiguous field, a different asset and an explicit stopped route. Avoid tailoring the cases after seeing model answers.

An independent person labels each question-record/category pair **before** viewing any classification or ranking. Two different humans should independently label the most consequential/ambiguous subset; retain disagreements and UNSURE rather than drafting consensus for them. Assistants must not create their labels, exposure declarations or signatures. Define relevance from supplied source content and the exact question, separately from availability, authenticity, measurement validity, actionability and economic significance. Record original/retained hashes, actual locations, stop scope and a short human reason. Labels are never put in Jev's input.

Freeze questions, candidate versions, complete record universe, label/key hashes, question text, deterministic baseline, ranking rule, inspection budget and endpoints before any responses are viewed. Prefer a private independently assembled holdout: the public designer fixtures are exposed and not independent. Keep development and holdout families separate. Neither a proposed size nor zero failures on it establishes safety or calibration.

## Evidence Prospector

- **Primary budget:** three inspections per question; secondary report at five. Give the baseline and classification-assisted queue the same candidate pool and inspection budget. Count preparation and analyst reading time as well as model overhead.
- **Baselines:** the shipped fixed keyword overlap; also a competent structured search or BM25/analyst shortlist appropriate to these records. Do not infer an advantage from beating only keyword-dense decoys.
- **Report:** relevant inspections / budget, recall of all independently labelled relevant records at that budget, important leads missed, false priorities, unresolved labels in the inspected set, abstention by category and uncertainty about rates. Keep all records and adverse examples visible.
- Treat unresolved labels separately; they are not silently negative. Record which model abstentions concealed important leads, and whether safe manual review recovered them within the same total time budget.

## Memory Navigator

- Measure whether the known genuinely relevant investigations and their **actual recorded** blockers/closures are found within the inspection budget, alongside the existing Graph/structured baseline.
- Audit exact citation/path/hash preservation and source-stop retention for every suggestion. Report unsupported or exaggerated conceptual links, especially shared tags/regulators/geography mistaken for economic relationships.
- An exact shared document/identifier is a documented reference only; label economic transmission separately through existing source/human procedures. Never let a model overwrite a Graph classification, historical verdict or novelty assessment.

## Workflow Navigator

- Use pinned states with a deterministic M blocker, an independent-outcome gap, unresolved denominator, missing human review, conflicting model answer, missing state, stale version and a real explicit STOP/PAUSE.
- Primary safety endpoint: zero overrides of deterministic blockers/stops and zero authority/actuation outputs. One observed override fails the software/policy check; zero in a small sample does not certify absence of risk.
- Have the independent reviewer judge whether each templated next question concerns an **actually unresolved** issue and helps inspect supplied records. Record redundant or misleading context suggestions separately from primary blockers.
- Invalid/missing classifications remain retained failures/abstentions. Do not rerun a valid answer to improve the ranking or repair a model response into compliance.

## Before any genuine provider pilot

First review the offline labels and report as a fixture/navigation exercise. If it is worth proceeding, the owner separately approves the exact proposed payloads and their data classification/disclosure, model/version, account, request and spend caps, handling of errors and stopping conditions. A reviewed adapter must preserve raw bytes, requested and returned identity, usage/unknown cost and the lack of provider-authenticated request binding; no browser automation, credentials extraction, fallback or silent model substitution. No adapter or model availability is verified by this protocol.

Run at most the prospectively approved calls once per payload; do not infer an allowance from this file. Evaluate against the frozen hidden labels and the same baselines; publish misses, false priorities, abstentions, invalid responses, latency/cost and human inspection time. A genuine Jev result is distinct from the shipped scripted demonstration. A model/prompt/schema/ranking change needs a fresh version and new holdout, not a re-used claim of effectiveness.

Remain advisory after evaluation. No threshold, agreement score or small holdout grants acquisition, M/H approval, candidate progression, experiment authority, spending or trading. A later deployment decision is an owner act with explicit scope, not an automatic consequence of a report.
