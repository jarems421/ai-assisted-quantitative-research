## Why the earlier selection process kept failing

The recurring problem is **selecting an appealing interpretation before locating a record of its distinguishing variable**. Sources about an event are plentiful; sources about the affected party's feasible response or protected cash exposure often are not.

| Closed investigation | Established | Distinguishing information not established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web adoption | Historical reconstructed labels were accessible. | Same-value detector lineage/public timing required by that historical design. This prevents its claimed actionable reconstruction, not every disclosed Level A use of reconstructed labels. |
| Salmon | Historical health records, visits and tracks. | Treatment task, independent service customers and available treatment access. Better timestamps alone cannot repair an unobserved task or booking right. |
| F02 | Material device dependence and an issuer-described provider-payment mechanism. | The proposed additional interpretation beyond closely related issuer/academic work. That does not prove market efficiency, but weakens the particular differentiation claim. |
| F03 | Use-specific permission and real replacement products. | Dated customer qualification and material, decision-sensitive substitution orders. Product capability cannot supply a missing customer commitment. |

These closures remain intact. A future selection process should require a **positive measurement control**: an original example that demonstrably records the proposed variable, its units and an independently measurable outcome. A purposive known example is legitimate for this purpose, provided it is disclosed and does not masquerade as an untouched performance sample. A neutral population sample belongs after the measurement exists. A quiet, random case cannot determine whether a field identifies a treatment or an agreement elsewhere.

Two further corrections matter. First, a contractual label or book balance is not the financial exposure: an agreement covers particular receivables; a tax-credit asset is not current tax relief. Second, predictor and outcome need matching legal entity, cohort, accounting basis and dates. A precise field measured on the wrong unit recreates the original problem.

Some obstacles are unavoidable: confidentiality, absent cohort allocation and uncertain historical public clocks. Others are avoidable: demanding execution certification for a disclosed Level A question, treating a large public file as scientific inaccessibility, overfetching entire parent filings, or writing a large evidence package before deciding whether the variable exists. None justifies weakening Level B timing or old frozen specifications. Nor should selection require proof of market inefficiency before an exploratory test; it requires a specific, falsifiable increment and a credible measurement path.

## F19: what the original records actually establish

### A material cost can land outside the failed insurer's obvious business

Aetna's June 2017 10-Q reports that Penn Treaty entered liquidation in March and that Aetna recognised **$231 million discounted liability and pretax expense**, against **$347 million undiscounted estimated assessments**, using **3.5%**. It did not recognise an expected premium-tax-offset asset for in-force business because the amount was not material. It also says historical assessments had ultimately been recovered by more than half through offsets. Those statements cannot be combined into a 50% forecast for the current liability. [Original Aetna filing, printed pp. 31–32](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1122304/000112230417000090/form10-q.htm).

This verifies real financial relevance to a listed medical insurer and distinguishes immediate expense, estimated future payments and eventual tax relief. It is management's reported accounting, not an independently estimated loss or proof of delayed investor understanding. The acquired filing was accepted **August 3, 2017**, after the expense had been recorded in the first quarter. We have not located a new, early information advantage by using it. [Original filing metadata](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1122304/000112230417000090/0001122304-17-000090-index.html).

### The outcome is recorded in an accounting system, rather than inferred from activity

The original NAIC Issue Paper 143R contains historical guidance requiring annual reconciliations of offset/surcharge assets, including increases, decreases and charge-offs. Its long-term-care insolvency disclosures distinguish discounted/undiscounted amounts and weighted payment/recovery periods. It also explains why certain statutory asset recognition can include expected short-term-health renewals, differing from GAAP treatment. That basis difference matters before comparing firms. The paper is not a current state tax-law or actual tax-capacity certificate. [NAIC original, relevant pp. 7–10 and 37–40](https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/143_a.pdf).

The acquired Voya Retirement Insurance and Annuity Company quarterly original reports **$9,407,518** guaranty funds receivable/on deposit at March 31, 2018 and **$9,406,010** at year-end 2017. It supplies no detailed credit-use reconciliation in its assessment note. **The balance change is not credits used.** A missing quarterly reconciliation cannot be replaced by subtracting balances. [Original quarterly statement, PDF pp. 2 and 15](https://s21.q4cdn.com/836187199/files/doc_downloads/voya_download_list/Voya-Retirement-Insurance-and-Annuity-Company-1st-Quarter-Financials-2018.pdf).

The parent filing's retained byte prefix includes a dated named-subsidiary/dividends bridge and a guaranty-fund footnote reporting **$6 million liability and $19 million future premium-tax-credit asset**. It is an **incomplete filing prefix**, not a complete original. Held-for-sale, discontinued and accounting-basis scopes were not fully reconciled. Assets can relate to assessments already paid: an asset exceeding remaining liability does not establish a windfall or negative cash burden. Neither these amounts nor the subsidiary balance can be assigned to Aetna. [Original parent filing, relevant retained sections printed pp. 134–135 and 318](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1535929/000153592918000006/voyafinancialinc201710-kre.htm).

The possible advantage would be a material, correctly scoped difference beyond already disclosed accruals. Neither difference nor market delay is demonstrated. Long-duration payments make an annual business/cash-flow comparison more natural than a forced 5–20-session stock study.

## Decision, minimal next investigation and path to an experiment

**No candidate is ready for an economic investment pilot. Choose F19 only for the next measurement decision.** It has the best combination of material financial consequences and a recorded outcome route; it does not depend wholly on reconstructing private bookings or customer intentions. Its remaining predictor/outcome alignment must be established, not assumed.

The next concrete scope I recommend for separate approval is **two analyst-hours, six ordinary starts maximum, 80 MiB combined, zero paid services**, confined to the already identified 2017/2018 annual PDFs. Their known sizes total **76,069,415 bytes (72.55 MiB)**. No parent refetch, other insurer, price series, new crawler or economic evaluation. The current 30 MiB authority cannot cover these originals; the present stops remain closed.

The deliverable would be the two original assessment-note tables, their units and one entity/basis reconciliation. It must answer:

1. Are actual credits applied, revisions and charge-offs separately reported, with opening/closing balances reconciling?
2. Do the maturity/rights fields and actual-use fields refer to the same assessment cohort and legal entity?
3. Is there an observable capacity or timing predictor beyond a book balance and already disclosed management expectation?
4. Does that matched object carry material exposure at the historically linked listed parent?

If only mixed-cohort totals are available, reject the cohort/state-capacity forecast. Do not invent jurisdiction allocations or substitute asset changes for utilisation. If the object matches, the next proposal can freeze one annual Level A realisation comparison, an appropriate persistence/management-disclosure baseline and independent evaluation periods. It must reserve these already inspected examples as measurement controls and assess the actual number of independent parent/cohort observations before selecting statistics. Historical annual filing dates, not nominal year ends, would govern any later Level B extension.

**No economic experiment is specified now**, because the required predictor/outcome pair has not qualified. A complete hypothesis, fixed sample, outcome, baseline, controls, missingness rules and one-analysis ceiling belong after that short source decision, before outcome testing. Promising an empirical schedule or statistical sample before resolving the join would repeat the selection failure. A short-horizon return study would require separate surprise/timing evidence; an annual cash-relief result alone would not justify it.
