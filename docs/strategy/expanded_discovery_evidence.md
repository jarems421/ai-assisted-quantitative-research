## Coverage and method

The review searched twenty relationships across distinct operational systems and industries. Primary evidence means an agency, data owner, original issuer, industry body, author/institution or original journal/publisher source; it does not mean we acquired the underlying scientific dataset. Commercial sources establish advertised functions and intended users. No purported customer totals, proprietary accuracy or returns were independently audited.

Browser work comprised **22 calls: 69 search queries, 14 direct opens, two link clicks and five finds within documents**. Underlying HTTP fetch/retry counts are unknown. Search results included secondary material and acronym collisions; adopted factual claims use the primary links in the map/deep dives. Search-index dates are not treated as record publication dates. No exhaustive literature/private-fund search is claimed. Every exact signal's private competitive use remains UNKNOWN.

The initial sweep examined records from CMS, BarentsWatch, EMA/EDQM, ECHA, FSIS, CBP, USCG, DOL, CourtListener, FERC, USGS, NOAA, Taiwan's water authority, UK Home Office, CelesTrak, ATF, UKAS, NOLHGA and Georgia 811. Follow-ups examined original academic work, issuer examples and commercial substitutes. All twenty have an identified original source; this does not imply equally deep research on all twenty or verified history/API access. No previous seven-candidate lead was counted toward the twenty.

## Documentation facts that constrain the recommendation

- **Aquaculture:** health/register and vessel history have different starts; reports arrive late; export zero/empty ambiguity exists. The developer page distinguishes `shipinfo` commercial rights and asks for serial access. The [general API terms](https://www.barentswatch.no/en/articles/api-terms-and-conditions/) do not override endpoint exceptions and warn about high-load handling fees. The OpenAPI page rendered as an empty shell through the browser; no schema methods, account access or historical data retrieval were certified.
- **CMS:** current-version downloads differ from old-version/API/archive access; posted/effective dates differ. Modifier/setting rules constrain what a payment edit means. No edit files, claims data or patient-level records were downloaded.
- **Chemicals:** a source listing and consortium table can reveal a use/holder relationship without establishing the legally controlling historical text or material revenue. One final-decision PDF was browser-denied. No entitlement or complete decision archive was established.
- **Drug inputs:** a rolling CEP action page is not a historical census, certificate count is not independent physical capacity, and regulator restrictions can have exceptions. No certificate database export or product/customer join was acquired.
- **Credit:** contributed RECAP coverage and upload timing differ from court availability. Neither a gross claim nor a docket name establishes a supplier loss or current equity identity. No claims/register corpus or paid PACER request was made.

These are discovered design constraints, not new operational stops on an authorized acquisition campaign: no such campaign was launched.
