## Two important timing qualifications

An [official December 2017 release](https://www.barentswatch.no/artikler/viser-bronnbater-i-fiskehelse/) describes roughly 70 registered wellboats with tracks back to 2012. Its historical visit rule was proximity within 400 metres at less than one knot. The current schema allows polygon-based proximity analysis; that older rule is not assumed constant across every year. The [2020 expansion](https://www.barentswatch.no/en/articles/updates-in-the-fishhealth-service/) and current vesseltrack description place wider coverage, including other farm-visiting vessels, from week 35/2020. The earlier discovery's 2020 reference was therefore incomplete as a description of all wellboat history. This qualification is newly recorded here; old reports and receipts retain their original bytes. Neither documentation statement proves that every old weekly API response is complete today.

Måsøval's [May 15, 2024 announcement](https://www.masoval.no/en/announcements/masoval-as-increases-wellboat-capacity/) describes its wholly owned service subsidiary signing a three-year Njord Kya charter, with options, while the boat was under construction and expected by year-end. Its [April 4, 2025 commissioning account](https://www.masoval.no/en/artikler/njord-kya-christened-at-siholmen-strengthens-wellboat-capacity-at-masoval/) describes freshwater/Thermolicer capability and use across smolt deployment through harvest. These verify an **illustrative contract/capability bridge**, not an actual task, exclusive treatment slot, cross-owner sharing or neutral sample observation. Both postdate week 10/2024 and cannot supply prior knowledge for either proposed sample date. The disagreement between expected delivery and the later commissioning account is retained rather than backfilled.

Weekly measurement, reporting deadlines, nightly updates and retrieval dates remain distinct. A current response's historical week or `version` field alone would not establish its historical publication time. Uncertain first-public timing can limit Level B while leaving some Level A research useful; unobserved task identity or physical availability is a separate measurement problem at either level.
