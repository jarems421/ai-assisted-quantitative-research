## EF06 v2 — explicitly rejected gross-only reformulation

After inspecting the originals, a narrower object is measurable: the HCI group’s reported new Citizens gross book, matched at annual/Q4 group scope to the separately reported USD 23m gross earned component. **M passes for that restricted descriptive object; it does not pass EF06 v1.** Keep management’s approximate counts, agency exact counts, November/December descriptions and gross/annualised/earned units separate. There is no policy-level or monthly equality certificate.

**REJECT further investment priority.** This changes the claim’s granularity and financial basis, and the issuer already explains the book and its revenue contribution. It is not an untouched candidate, evidence of prediction or permission for a generic premium-volume pilot. The ledger preserves v1 and the post-observation v2 decision separately.
