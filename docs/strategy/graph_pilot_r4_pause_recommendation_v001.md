# Graph Pilot R4: recommendation to pause (version 001)

> **A recommendation, not a decision.** The owner has not adopted it. It changes no experiment status, frozen record or historical conclusion, and it activates, cancels or authorises nothing. R4 remains **NOT_RUN**.

## Recommendation

**Pause Graph R4 pending a future owner decision.** No further R4 preparation, preflight, recruitment or activation should follow automatically.

## Basis

From the [pre-activation feasibility audit](graph_pilot_r4_feasibility_audit_v001.md), classified `INCONCLUSIVE_MORE_OFFLINE_EVIDENCE_NEEDED`:

- **Observation-first** has no documented route with confirmed terms. EIA and ERCOT need keys. CAISO's E2 is ambiguous. FDIC financial data is excluded as outcome-bearing under the existing rule.
- **Relationship-first** has one plausible environment, GLEIF, under the recommended strict freshness rule.
- **Event-first** has two documented candidates (CPSC, then FDIC history), both with open questions.
- **Three comparable pairs** therefore depend on single environments. No candidate is established as E1–E8 eligible.
- **Two independent human reviewers** would each need an estimated 6–30 hours, and none is recruited. That cost is high relative to a descriptive result from three or four pairs, given that R3 judged every graph-operator connection trivial.

## What stays unchanged

- R4 remains NOT_RUN and unauthorised.
- The R4 protocol draft, the Phase A and Phase B engineering and the operational package are kept as they are. They stay drafts with no experimental authority.
- R1–R3 records, results and classifications are unchanged, including R3 INCONCLUSIVE.
- Every persisted source stop, Measurement Gate M, EXP001–EXP007 and all other historical decisions are unchanged.
- Opportunity Scout is untouched.

## Options for a future owner decision

1. **Resume preparation.** Resolve the offline evidence in audit section 8, points 1–5 (an observation-first route, CAISO terms, the FDIC History definition file, GLEIF date filtering, CPSC `CompanyID`). Then secure two reviewers, and complete the activation template.
2. **Redesign** with a smaller or differently scoped evaluation. That would need a new protocol and a new owner decision.
3. **Stop R4**, and record why.

Any of these needs an explicit owner instruction. This recommendation is not one.
