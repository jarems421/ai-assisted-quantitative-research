# Measurement Gate M: prove the research object before deep investigation

**2026-10-21. Governing rule for new, separately authorised frontier work.** M is a pre-Level-A researchability gate, **not a fourth evidence level, an economic result or permission to run a study**. It formalises the positive-control principle already recorded in [the bottleneck review](discovery_bottleneck_report.md), using the [owner's new instruction](owner_measurement_gate_campaign_2026-10-21.md). Historical decisions, gates and frozen experiments are not migrated or relabelled.

Use this order: **short economic sketch → exact distinctive variable → original historical measurement control → matched outcome → material exposure → timing and horizon → closest substantive substitute → deeper research**. A brief substitute check may occur earlier when it can cheaply eliminate a redundant claim. Do not write an extensive mechanism essay to compensate for an unobserved predictor.

## The six requirements

| Requirement | Evidence necessary for M | What fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distinctive predictor | One actual historical original records the quantity/right/constraint that makes this claim different. Identify source, date, field/passage and version. | Related industry data, a schema, approval, contract title or asset visit without the required quantity. |
| Meaning and units | Record units, denominator, granularity, scope and whether the value is measured, reported, estimated or conditional. | Nameplate as spare capacity; accounting balance as utilisation; gross liability as net retained cost. |
| Separate outcome | Identify a separately recorded economic outcome and demonstrate a historical same-unit join or clearly evidenced disclosure route. Record an actual outcome value where available; a route-only result stays provisional rather than a full pass. | The predictor repeated as its own outcome; a theoretical payment formula presented as independently observed cash. |
| Valid join | Match legal entity, economic cohort, geography, period and accounting basis as required. Predictor and outcome may have different physical units, such as quantities and revenue, provided the economic exposure matches. | Invented jurisdiction/claim allocations, current ownership backfill or a convenient aggregate replacing the specified cohort. |
| Public-time route | Distinguish economic occurrence, first public availability, dataset timestamp and research retrieval/reconstruction. There must be a plausible, identified route to public-version evidence. | Inferring original publication from today's old-dated row. Exact historical public timing can remain unresolved at M and Level A. |
| Financial bridge | A dated listed-parent relationship and credible financial scale on the relevant object. Record gross and net amounts separately. | Industry relevance, a ticker-name match or group revenue assigned to an unmeasured product. |

All six must be evidenced for **M PASSED**. There is no compensating weighted score. Unknowns remain UNVERIFIED, with a concrete reason. A separately measured outcome need not come from a different publisher, but it must measure the consequence independently of our predictor construction. Issuer forecasts and attributed causal explanations are not realised outcomes. A legally specified cash amount can establish entitlement without establishing collection.

A historical positive control is deliberately selected to check whether measurement exists. Label it **purposive / already inspected** and retain it outside later untouched evaluation. M does not certify population completeness, independence, power, causality, predictive increment or profitability. A famous case is not a sample-size certificate.

## Keep measurement, timing and investment usefulness separate

M asks whether a study object exists. Level A tests a declared retrospective economic proposition with honest reconstruction/version limits. Level B additionally establishes what information and mapping were producible at each historical decision time. Level C adds executable securities, costs, liquidity, lifecycle handling and independent investment evidence. Passing one never activates the next.

For investment priority also require **H: a concrete expectation-revision event plausibly operating within 5–20 trading sessions**. Distinguish this window from the time over which the business earns revenue or pays a liability. A quarterly outcome can fit H if new information changes near-term expectations; an annual release date alone does not establish that channel. H is a reasoned design assessment, not evidence that prices actually adjust slowly. Do not inspect returns to rescue it.

Before recommending a study, write three separate propositions: economic effect, informational lead and residual investment value. State the nearest baseline and what the new inference adds. Existing professional interest can validate relevance while increasing competition. Raw-data novelty, mechanism novelty, signal construction, cross-source inference and remaining market inefficiency are five different claims; missing research and UNKNOWN private use earn no credit.

## A lightweight upstream record

Use one campaign ledger and concise cards, with stable family/claim-version IDs. Record exact predictor/outcome; sources and attempts; units and four clocks; exposure/materiality; outcome join; related old claims; closest substitute/baseline; falsifier; request/byte/time expenditure; status and reason. Link immutable receipts rather than duplicating raw bodies. Existing stage/trial systems remain untouched.

Every targeted source attempt receives a reservation before dispatch and an outcome afterward, including failures. Keep status history append-only within the record. A changed distinguishing variable, population, unsupported proxy or materially different mechanism becomes a new claim version and retains the old failure. Clarifying a field without changing the claim can be a documented correction. Brainstorming is not a statistical evaluation; a source check is a discovery attempt, and neither is automatically a backtest trial. Later inference must account for selection history rather than pretending the survivor was the only idea considered.

Use these dispositions:

- **REJECT:** specified economic, observation, competition or structural failure. State the scope; do not infer that the whole industry has no useful data.
- **HOLD:** a decisive measurement or horizon problem remains; no automatic source work.
- **VERIFY:** one specified, cheap additional observation can resolve the main uncertainty, within existing authority or as an explicit proposal.
- **M PASSED:** all six measurement requirements evidenced; no predictive claim or next-stage authority.
- **LEVEL A DESIGN CANDIDATE:** M plus a credible population, materiality, baseline and worthwhile economic question justify a separately approved design. Being least weak is insufficient.

## Acquisition and implementation limits

Metadata and relevant indexes should precede large originals. A finite, decisive large file can justify a proposed exception; **only an owner-approved task allowance permits it**. Tiered acquisition is a planning heuristic, not a new default spending or byte authorisation. The F19 80 MiB proposal remains unapproved. Do not use another task's unused starts or weaken a persisted stop.

Keep originals private/immutable with source locator, retrieval UTC, status, bytes/hash, completeness, rights limitation, transformation provenance and clock interpretation. Count redirects, corrected requests and errors. A retained prefix is incomplete evidence, never a full document. Stop on access denials; no bypass or confidential contact. Keep browser-index evidence distinct from archived original bytes and disclose unknown underlying browser transports/cost.

This implements a documentation rule and a lightweight discovery ledger convention. It adds **no new Store transition, orchestrator, schema service, paid integration, automatic gate evaluator or portfolio infrastructure**. Remote branch protection, signed release policy, external registration and broader provenance test additions remain separate proposals, not changes enacted by this rule.
