### The HCI gross control: a pass for a different, rejected claim

The data do demonstrate a narrower relationship: **HCI's reported 2023 acquired gross book → its separately reported 2023/Q4 Citizens gross earned component**. That is a legitimate original historical measurement object at group/cohort scope. Its quantities, units, outcome, parent scale and public-source route are evidenced; it needs no allocation of premiums to individual carriers. Accordingly **EF06 v2 passes M for this restricted gross object**.

This is not an original v1 success. The new version changes both granularity and financial basis, was formed after seeing the observations, and is recorded as such. Agency exact counts and management's approximation are retained without an invented numerical tolerance or policy-level equality claim. The annual source is a labelled prefix with complete relevant Note 1/13 passages and unit headers, **not a complete filing or full accounting audit**. First-public times and the November/December accounting/assumption distinction remain unresolved; the pass does not certify a monthly conversion forecast or Level B actionability.

**Reject v2 for further investment priority.** The issuer already describes the acquired book and premium contribution. No differentiated retained-margin information, public lead or residual repricing mechanism was established. A measurable descriptive accounting relationship does not earn an economic pilot simply because other candidates failed. No regression, premium forecast or profit estimate was calculated.

## Recommended next decision: stop acquisition

**Do not launch Level A, repeat this scan, activate a backup or approve F19 from this result.** The main uncertainty for the best newly measured family is whether carrier/cohort retention, cancellations and reinsurance translate assumed book size into a materially different retained outcome *before* contemporaneous issuer interpretation. We did not identify an original with that information or a distinctive public lead.

If a future owner decision revisits EF06 v1, the first requirement should be an **already identified public carrier/cohort retention-and-premium reconciliation**, plus its release/version evidence and the contemporaneous HCI assumption announcement. Cap a conditional document inspection at **one analyst-hour, three starts, 5 MiB, zero paid access**, once those exact originals are identified. This is an upper bound for a possible future decision, **not a recommended or authorised hunt for new sources**. Pass only if it supplies the original net object and a meaningful information increment; fail if it gives only group gross premiums, requires allocations or repeats an existing disclosure. A failure ends that formulation without a gross-only fallback.

At present no such decisive original is located, so the smallest justified additional acquisition budget is **zero**. This is preferable to another speculative source campaign. Any later discovery direction should start from an encountered record of a real distinctive quantity rather than another mechanism ranking. The gate and ledger are implemented; prospective economic experiments, broader acquisition tiers, platform/governance work and strategy deployment remain unapproved.
