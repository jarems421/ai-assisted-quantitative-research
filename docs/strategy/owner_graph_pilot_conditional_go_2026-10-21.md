## 2. WHAT WE ARE TESTING

Two separate research questions:

**Q1 — Discovery methods**

Under equal budgets, what do the four discovery entry methods produce?

- Observation-first
- Relationship-first
- Event-first
- Hypothesis-first

Report the results descriptively, recognising that each method uses a different starting packet.

**Q2 — Graph contribution**

Within the same frozen starting packet, does Discovery Lab plus Graph v1 perform better than Discovery Lab plus competent structured search?

Both approaches have the same source access, starting evidence, available historical memory and analyst-time budget.

We want to measure:

- Verified economically meaningful connections per analyst-hour
- Incorrect or premature economic joins
- Time required to evaluate useful connections
- Time to decisive falsification
- Previously recorded dead-end research avoided
- Original frozen M passes
- Separate H assessments
- Additional analyst and adjudication burden

Do not equate graph-generated suggestions with verified discoveries.

A connection found by both approaches is not graph-only.

A useful explanation, earlier falsification or avoided error may still count as a graph benefit, even when there is no novel connection.

No investment advantage, price prediction or alpha claim is authorised.
