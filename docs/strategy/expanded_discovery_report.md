## What changed in the investigation

Rather than looking for an obscure series that might correlate with returns, the search examined situations where **the actor named in a headline is not the actor who ultimately bears the constraint or receives the cash**. The strongest ideas ask about feasible substitutes, protected access or contractual incentives:

- A farmer may face the same biological pressure as a rival but have worse access to treatment vessels.
- A device can remain covered while a complementary provider service becomes less profitable, changing adoption incentives.
- Permission to keep using an incumbent chemical can help its customer while postponing a substitute supplier's expected sales.
- Several drug-input certificates can conceal one common restricted production dependency.
- A supplier's gross bankruptcy claim can differ greatly from its unprotected liquidity exposure.

These are hypotheses about economic transmission, not claims that combining files creates an advantage. Several initially attractive leads were demoted after finding direct commercial substitutes or missing exposure information. Nuclear, FAA, river, MSHA, PTAB, EPA and NLRB are not counted among the twenty. No existing source stop was bypassed.

## Why F01 is the recommendation

F01 has an identifiable biological business, a proposed physical bottleneck with public contracting examples, multiple listed owners and a short operational response horizon. It also supplies a clean way for the idea to be wrong: if shared vessel demand adds nothing beyond own health and ordinary spatial infection, the supposed extra relationship is unnecessary. That is stronger than testing another lice indicator against a stock price.

The potential differentiation is economically specific: **ability to act under a shared constraint**, not superior knowledge that a problem exists. Access protection can reverse a simple inference from a regional outbreak. A supply shock can raise the product price while destroying one producer's economics, so the eventual financial question concerns relative exposure and mitigation capacity. This recommendation is not based on the easiest endpoint or a claim that aquaculture investors are inattentive. It survives substantial close prior art but still faces a serious commercial-information disadvantage.

The question differs from F02/F03 in having potentially repeated weekly operational evidence rather than primarily a limited sequence of legal/policy decisions. Those weeks are not independent events, and there are few listed parents; this is a feasibility consideration, not an effective-sample or power certificate. No fixed investment universe, return feature, threshold or holding rule is selected here.

## Practical initial source-verification plan — proposed only

The next step would verify whether F01's *distinctive inputs* exist and can be used. It would not run a model, compute a lice signal, inspect stock returns or acquire a full coastline panel. **No part of this plan has executed.** It requires a separate bounded instruction.

| Step | Small observable deliverable | Stop or limitation |

Record whether each required component permits this use at no charge. | Registration is not entitlement. No purchase, trial signup, commercial `shipinfo` use or alternate-provider rescue. If identifying suitable services requires unavailable rights, stop. |
| **2. Historical joins** | After freezing exact routes and a neutral frame, inspect at most **12 site-weeks**: six deterministically selected sites in two Norwegian production areas, weeks 10/2021 and 10/2024. Retain missing sites and changes of owner. Establish actual date coverage rather than extrapolating health history to visits. | No replacement of missing/quiet sites with famous outbreaks. No extension to a different decade, country or generic biology study if the service history is inadequate. |
| **3. Task and resource meaning** | For no more than two evidenced shared vessels, reconcile visit fields with contemporaneous treatment records and one original capability or charter source. Distinguish a treatment task from harvest/smolt transport and mere passage. | AIS visits are not spare slots. If the available fields cannot distinguish these roles, do not claim a congestion measure. No expensive hand-built fleet warehouse. |
| **4. Economic owner and access protection** | Check two historical permit-holder/parent bridges and at most two original charter/access disclosures. Record own capacity versus shared dependence only where evidenced; keep unknowns. | No current-owner backfill, inferred biomass from licensed capacity or claim that a generic time charter guarantees the specific treatment slot. |
| **5. Timing and revisions** | Preserve measurement week, submitted/updated/public fields where present and retrieval time. Determine whether valid retrospective reconstruction is possible and whether a later Level B route exists. | Unknown first-public time limits historical actionability; it does not automatically invalidate an honest Level A mechanism study. If the essential physical relationship itself is unobservable, stop. |
| **6. Return for a decision** | Report actual inspected counts, rights, field examples, mapping successes/failures, historical overlap, costs and the precise unresolved link. Recommend or decline a concrete economic experiment. | No hypothesis test, market outcomes, automatic backup or full acquisition. Do not turn an API success into economic evidence. |

Suggested ceiling for that future verification: **two analyst-days, 30 ordinary starts including documentation/corrections, 20 MiB total bodies**, serial respectful access with at least one second between starts, no automatic retries. Small field/range requests only; do not truncate a huge download and call it a bounded sample. An access denial ends that route. Do not contact providers or submit credentials/registrations without applicable authorization. These are proposed constraints, not a fresh allowance consumed now.

A passing result would establish observable, usable service/health/ownership inputs and a credible financial bridge, not an edge. A failure would identify the missing distinctive input. There is no preapproved next candidate if it fails. Actual time, costs and counts would replace the estimates; the currently linked cloud account supplies no spending authority.
