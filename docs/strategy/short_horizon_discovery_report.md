# Short-horizon discovery: ten hypotheses, one justified measurement challenge

**2026-10-21 — Discovery complete; no economic pilot recommended.** Of ten additional hypotheses, **offshore cleanup obligations returning to former owners (SH09)** warrants one small verification decision. It has an unusually concrete positive measurement control: an original issuer record connects particular failed asset sales, a former owner's lease population, public engineering estimates and a subsequent material loss. Whether those inputs could have supported a useful inference **before** the issuer's announcement remains unestablished. The other nine do not presently justify further acquisition.

**F19 remains the existing leading measurement candidate, unapproved and closed.** This review neither activates its proposed annual-note investigation nor replaces its historical disposition. SH09 is a challenger to verify, not a newly approved successor. No candidate here has demonstrated predictive performance, delayed stock-price incorporation or a remaining profitable opportunity.

## What changed the assessment

The most informative discoveries were often close substitutes. Published BDC research and marketed loan-matching tools already examine shared credit exposures. A restaurant-data provider explicitly interprets corporate/franchise structures and refranchising alongside Texas alcohol receipts. An energy vendor explicitly models subsidy-vintage negative-price losses. These findings support economic relevance while narrowing our proposed differentiation; they are not evidence that every related investment question is exhausted.

The offshore case is stronger than a generic regulatory-event story because the economically important event is **which productive assets fail to find a new responsible buyer**, rather than merely the operator entering bankruptcy. An old seller can become exposed despite having no current production from those assets. Professional analysis already understands this mechanism. The possible research contribution is a timely, correctly scoped estimate of the *change* in that seller's residual burden. Its feasibility and incremental information still need testing.

These are ten fixed source questions from the pushed plan, not ten selected winners. Some relate to familiar broad families: slot-retention rights differ from the earlier FAA propagation proposal; offshore predecessor obligations differ from F09 supplier receivables; refinery report revisions are a narrower question than the earlier EPA production lead. None reopens those investigations. The list covers credit, property finance, environmental credits, restaurants, education, satellite payments, aviation, refining, offshore obligations and electricity contracts.

The raw sources are familiar and **no new economic mechanism is claimed**. Signal-construction and cross-source differentiation are separate hypotheses evaluated below, often weakened by an observed close substitute. Neither would establish remaining profitability. All ten exact signals' private competitive usage remains UNKNOWN. The most plausible friction is translating a legally specific change into retained financial exposure before a company update; specialists may already resolve that translation, so its persistence must be tested rather than assumed.

### SH01 — A borrowing company is not a shared claim

The valuable inference would concern contractual cash income at the later-reporting lender, not merely a difference between two discretionary fair values. Original BDC schedules are a concrete route to lender, borrower, instrument, rate, principal and fair value. Boston Fed researchers describe constructing these objects and already investigate the predictive information in BDC disclosures. Their paper is close prior art, not an economic result produced here. [Boston Fed, August 2026](https://www.bostonfed.org/publications/current-policy-perspectives/2026/early-warnings-private-credit-bdc-portfolios.aspx).

The competitive comparison is particularly demanding: [Kanonstone](https://www.kanonstone.com/) advertises loan-level co-holder matching; [Oxford Ledge](https://www.oxfordledge.com/bdcs/borrowers/) exposes cross-BDC borrowers; [MarkQuality](https://www.markquality.io/research/bdc-marks/2025q3) publishes peer-mark comparisons. Their advertised coverage/accuracy is not audited here. Nie's *Funding Frictions and the Valuation of Private Credit* and Andrade/Lie's *Valuation, Liquidity, and Fund Structure in Private Credit* explicitly compare shared claims; original paper search excerpts, rather than full methods, were inspected. [Nie](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/7479079.pdf?abstractid=7479079&mirid=1), [Andrade/Lie](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/7533698.pdf?abstractid=7533698&mirid=1&type=2).

**Increment to test:** a newly disclosed cessation of cash interest, available before the co-holder reports, could be more informative about its impending income than an already-known valuation gap. Neither the calendar lead nor an exact pre-restructuring claim was verified. A search returned a complaint alleging Pluralsight mark discrepancies; different quarters and possible restructurings make that unsuitable as a validated same-claim control. No allegation of misvaluation is adopted.

**Cheapest falsification:** inspect one pair of original schedules and disclosure clocks. Reject the proposed lead if maturity, seniority, restructuring lineage or observation date differs, if the second holder already reports the change, or if the closest obtainable product already supplies the same timely cash-status inference. Later Level A outcomes would be cash income/nonaccrual, with holder size and pre-existing borrower distress as controls; fair-value convergence alone would not validate it. Materiality must use the affected holder's actual position and income, not the borrower's total debt.

### SH02 — A covenant threshold does not measure trapped cash

Original securitisation terms specify debt-yield triggers, lease-renewal conditions and, in another deal, cash/letter-of-credit cure alternatives. They positively establish that the legal mechanics exist. They do not establish a trigger occurred, a listed borrower owned that asset, or how much distributable cash was retained. [2016 terms](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/850779/000153949716002327/n620_fwp-x5.htm), [2024 terms](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/850779/000153949724002391/n4545-x6_ts.htm).

ALX's original accounts identify 731 Lexington Avenue and its mortgage, but consolidated restricted cash includes multiple purposes. The reviewed records did not join one actual sweep/reserve balance to a following distribution. [ALX 2024 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/3499/000000349925000004/alx-20241231.htm). A separate AMT disclosure is a useful caution: it publishes cash-trap thresholds, actual distributions and substantial coverage headroom, so the existence of a securitisation is not itself an adverse signal. It is not our property-level trigger control. [AMT Q1 2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1053507/000105350724000084/amt-20240331.htm).

**Closest substitute/counterargument:** loan servicer disclosures and CMBS surveillance are the natural substitutes; no particular commercial product's ability to supply this exact distribution inference was verified within the budget. That UNKNOWN earns no advantage credit. Debt service, voluntary cure, refinancing, tenant reserves and default can explain cash retention differently. A fee or reserve can be financially important without predicting equity repricing.

**Cheapest falsification:** require one original loan report with an actual trigger and segregated retained balance, its contractual release/cure terms, and a same-borrower cash-distribution outcome. Reject if only generic covenants or consolidated restricted cash are public. No servicer corpus is justified now.

### SH03 — Retroactive carbon-credit rights, rather than gas production

Montauk's March 28, 2023 release says Pico's Tier 2 pathway was certified March 27 with provisional CI **−260.56**, usable for fuel quantities from **Q4 2022**. This is a positive example of a regulatory decision changing the treatment of earlier production. It is not proof of issued credits, realised proceeds or the producer's retained share. The issuer itself explains the retroactivity, weakening a hidden-interpretation claim. [Original Montauk release](https://ir.montaukrenewables.com/news-releases/news-release-details/montauk-renewables-inc-announces-approval-provisional-california).

The CARB-indexed B0369 application supplies a named facility and operating/model context, but direct opening produced a JavaScript verification gate. The application was not independently read in full or promoted to certification evidence. [CARB original application route](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/classic/fuels/lcfs/fuelpathways/comments/tier2/b0369_report.pdf). Eligibility, generation, issuance, sale and accounting recognition must remain separate.

**Increment to test:** whether a previously unrecognised, company-retained eligible backlog adds to the next disclosed credit receipts beyond the certified CI and the issuer's own explanation. The business effect can enter a quarter; a 5–20-session opportunity would require a separately established public CARB release leading the issuer or results. No such lead was established. The closest inspected substitute is precisely the issuer announcement and regulatory application; a specialist commercial backlog model or closely matching academic study was not verified. Private usage stays UNKNOWN.

**Cheapest falsification:** locate one existing project disclosure separating eligible prior fuel, contractually retained credits and realised project credits/revenue. Reject if the only outcome is combined RNG revenue, if the economic share is private, or if the alleged surprise is already in management's release. Credit-price movements, meter verification and counterparty sharing are central alternative explanations. No volume × CI × spot-price pseudo-revenue should be constructed.

Its First Watch analysis also warns that alcohol is a small part of total sales. These are very close advertised interpretations, not proof of audited data or profitable fund use. [Original conference paper](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2025/program/paper/nzRBfkas).

**Cheapest falsification:** for one disclosed ownership change, require dated control/royalty terms, retained-location receipts and a material share of parent sales reported before earnings. Reject if brand-level demand is all we can measure or ownership-aware aggregation merely reproduces the provider. Alcohol mix, local demand, tax amendments and late filings require controls. No parent-sales nowcast or original-row sample was acquired, and the searched commercial numerical claims are not our observations.

### SH05 — The additional funding condition matters more than the monitoring label

Corinthian's June 19, 2014 8-K reports **HCM1 plus an additional 21-day waiting period**, versus ordinary drawdown availability of 24–72 hours, and explicitly warns of insufficient liquidity and unsuccessful lender financing. Thus a monitoring category alone would miss a material timing condition. This is a positive disclosed cash mechanism, not our discovery of a hidden crisis. [Original 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1066134/000110465914046978/a14-15736_18k.htm).

FSA distinguishes HCM1 drawdown mechanics from HCM2 reimbursement requests; neither an institution's label nor student aid originations provides its actual drawdown delay and cash balance. [Original FSA explanation](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/fsa-print/publication/1003813). School/OPEID, owner at the time, the added condition, disbursement amounts and available liquidity must join before measuring a funding gap. Other listed education companies are possible exposures, not inferred to share Corinthian's condition.

**Closest substitute/counterargument:** the contemporaneous issuer warning already states the proposed cash-flow inference; subsequent legal/policy material also analyses Corinthian. No exact commercial funding-gap model or broader academic predictive design was certified. Regulatory misconduct and prospective closure are severe confounders; a single extreme distressed company does not establish a new event population. The short window is plausible for a new condition's disclosure, while a further 5–20-day price delay is unproved.

**Cheapest falsification:** require one separately identified school condition publicly available ahead of its parent's cash warning and an independent reported shortfall/financing outcome. Stop if only monitoring labels or simultaneous issuer interpretation are available. Historical failed/delisted issuers must remain in any later population; choosing only today's survivors is impermissible. No source campaign is recommended now.

### SH06 — A milestone can release billions without changing recurring revenue

SES's August 10, 2023 announcement identifies FCC validation, **$2.99 billion** Phase II eligibility before stated tax of 18–19%, and expected Q4 receipt. It distinguishes the earlier Phase I payment. This is substantial cash entitlement and explicit management interpretation, not ordinary satellite sales. [Original SES release](https://www.ses.com/press-release/ses-completes-fccs-c-band-transition-clearing-and-relocation-plan-us).

The original FCC order is identified and search excerpts describe validation and payer/clearinghouse deadlines, but direct opening returned 403. No bypass or complete independent legal-performance verification followed. [FCC order route](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-23-674A1_Rcd.pdf). **Expected receipt is not observed receipt.** No cash-receipt series was acquired.

**Closest substitute/counterargument:** the issuer's financial announcement already calculates the cash benefit and timing. The programme's few phases share regulatory and payer risks; different operators are not automatically independent shocks. Remaining remediation, challenge, tax and timing provisions matter, but no overlooked condition was found. No exact academic equity rule or paid product was verified; absence is not favorable evidence.

**Cheapest falsification:** match validation to the earlier transition forecast and subsequent actual cash statement. Reject an information-advantage claim if the payment was already anticipated and promptly explained, or if no independent comparable milestones exist. This can be a useful accounting control without becoming a research priority.

### SH07 — A carrier's option to stop flying is not measured savings

The FAA's August 2023 notice permits conditional voluntary returns of up to 10% at specified airports and requires identification of the affected schedules. It reports United's petition and explains slot-retention pressure. This gives original evidence of a valuable operating option and informed industry attention. [FAA notice, issued August 9](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/NYC_Waiver_Extension_FAA%20_8-8-23.pdf).

**Increment to test:** whether carrier-specific accepted relief avoids low-contribution required flying beyond what demand, staffing and already-announced schedules predict. Actual accepted returns, dated slot holdings and carrier-level contribution economics were not inspected. A cancelled flight can displace revenue or passengers to another service, while aircraft may be redeployed. Gross cost-per-flight is not net savings.

**Closest substitute/counterargument:** carriers requested the relief themselves; published schedules and operational analysis are obvious substitutes. No specific commercial marginal-profit product or closely matching academic study was verified. This is not the earlier network-delay propagation hypothesis. A policy notice is public and may matter promptly; a month-long investor processing delay is not established.

**Cheapest falsification:** require one accepted return linked to the prior schedule and a separately observable operating response, then a defensible bound on material net economics. If only a waiver ceiling and later flight counts exist, reject the issuer-earnings forecast. No schedule, slot or flight dataset was acquired.

### SH08 — Reporting revisions compete with direct physical monitoring

TCEQ requires an initial notification within 24 hours of discovery above reporting thresholds and a final report within 14 days of the event ending. These are submission requirements, not guaranteed public-release times or a preserved revision history. [Original reporting guidance](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/airquality/emission-events). Emissions, flaring and lost production are different measurements.

**Increment to test:** a newly public failed-restart revision might reveal additional output loss beyond the initial outage. No original matched initial/final event or independent same-refinery throughput outcome was acquired. Regional utilisation cannot validate the specific unit. A repeated incident may refer to another unit or an emissions-control problem rather than an extended refinery shutdown.

**Closest substitute/counterargument:** Wood Mackenzie advertises unit-level refinery outage/restart monitoring and archived operating alerts; its product brochure specifically targets traders and analysts. Its claimed lead times, coverage and trading benefits were not independently audited. This is direct competition on the proposed information, potentially much faster than regulatory reports. [Original product brochure](https://www.woodmac.com/link/c9afd8939fd149038cd97b81ff849d26.aspx), [provider's current description](https://www.woodmac.com/industry/trading-analytics/trading/oil/).

**Cheapest falsification:** locate one preserved public revision and independently measured unit output. Reject if the record is overwritten, the only outcome repeats the same operator report, or specialist alerts already provide that revision faster. No claims about all TCEQ history or universal refinery-data inaccessibility follow.

### SH09 — Unsold productive assets can change a former owner's burden

Chevron's November 2024 response describes **134 Cox-estate leases with outstanding decommissioning obligations in its liability chain**. Management had expected producing properties to find buyers; the December sale covered only a subset. The letter identifies a **December 12, 2023 motion to abandon**, public BSEE cost estimates, and approximately **$170 million financial security** available at the bankruptcy filing. Those are concrete measurement objects, not an inferred booking or private customer intention. The source is a later explanation; it does not establish what outsiders could reconstruct in December 2023. [Original response, pp. 2–5](https://chevroncorp.gcs-web.com/static-files/51fe92ca-3680-42a4-b804-2c9433cfe667).

Chevron's August response says approximately 80% of a roughly **$1.9 billion after-tax Q4 2023 loss** related to Cox, with Fieldwood accounting for substantially the remainder, and explains the change from earlier uncertainty to recognition. This is reported financial materiality, not proof of investor surprise. Allocation, tax basis, security recoverability and estimate scope must be preserved; the charge is not cash paid that quarter. [Original August response, pp. 1–5](https://chevroncorp.gcs-web.com/static-files/35792537-c5a8-4fbd-8c90-377d15592e11).

The January 2, 2024 8-K disclosed the returning obligations alongside California impairments. Its **$3.5–4.0 billion combined after-tax charge estimate is not the cleanup charge alone**. Consequently even a later market study could not attribute that announcement's return mechanically to cleanup. [Original 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/93410/000009341024000002/cvx-20240102.htm).

An independent issuer example exists: APA's 2021 filing excerpt reports a **$1.2–1.4 billion undiscounted exposure range**, a **$740 million expected security-reimbursement asset** and a **$446 million loss** associated with legacy Gulf properties. These accounting amounts do not equal a simple liability-minus-security calculation; earlier balances and timing matter. The full filing opening exceeded the browser's size limit, so this is an original indexed excerpt, not a full-note audit. [Original APA filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1841666/000178403122000009/apa-20211231.htm). It corroborates a possible outcome route, not two statistically independent observations or a complete join.

**The proposed distinctive variable:** the dated change in predecessor exposure when an expected successor-funded asset moves into an abandoned/unfunded set, conditional on which obligations accrued during the predecessor's tenure and which recoverable security actually applies. Current ownership, gross engineering cost and an operator's total bankruptcy liabilities are inadequate substitutes. The economic forecast concerns a liability revision and long-duration future spending; the potential 5–20-session opportunity concerns processing a new sale/abandonment disclosure before the former owner's next announcement. Only that second clock fits the investment objective.

**Measurement route and its limit:** BSEE's current official query describes lease/well/platform/pipeline cost estimates, including P50/P70/P90, and explicitly says these do not identify liable companies or required financial assurance. Its current displayed date is not a 2023 cost vintage. BOEM/BSEE leasing documentation identifies assignments and serial-register records as separate routes. [BSEE cost-query documentation](https://www.data.bsee.gov/Leasing/DecomCostEst/Default.aspx), [leasing inventory](https://www.data.bsee.gov/main/leasing.aspx?op=Search). The current page opening showed navigation; substantive field descriptions came from the indexed official text. A later section request timed out. **No lease table, historical motion attachment, order, historical cost snapshot or bond/indemnity allocation was acquired and joined here.**

**Competition is close, not absent.** Boomhower's AER research studies bankruptcy, financial assurance and environmental risk in Texas oil/gas, not this proposed short-window predecessor estimate. The publisher abstract was read; no results were replicated. [Original AER abstract](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20160346). King & Spalding's own analysis discusses joint operating agreements and returning obligations; indexed substantive excerpts were inspected, while the opening mainly exposed navigation. [Original practitioner analysis](https://www.kslaw.com/insights/articles/the-role-of-joint-operating-agreements-in-managing-boomerang-liabilities-in-the-gulf-of-america). GOMDecom explicitly analyses the Cox title/security chain and markets decommissioning intelligence. That is a particularly close observed substitute, aimed in the inspected article at contractors; verified investment customers or a contemporaneous 2023 product were not established. [Provider's own analysis](https://gomdecom.com/blog/predecessor-liability-gulf-bankruptcy).

The remaining differentiation would have to be **as-of changes in net, financially material predecessor exposure beyond the contemporaneous issuer and obtainable specialist analysis**. These are previously accrued regulatory obligations; bankruptcy does not newly create them or let the court assign the regulator's liability. Lease mapping alone is familiar. Legal specialists may already perform the inference better, the charge may already be expected, and correlated bankruptcies may leave too few independent episodes. Oil prices, asset-sale proceeds, co-obligor solvency and unrelated impairments are important confounders. None is an assumed moat or a reason to claim novelty.

**Cheapest decisive falsification:** try to reconstruct one small part of the known Cox/Chevron chain using records actually public before January 2, 2024. Fail the short-horizon proposition if the essential liability/security mapping is only supplied by the later correspondence, if a contemporary substitute already supplies the estimate, or if the earlier public event adds no identifiable exposure change. A retrospective Level A reconstruction could remain possible after a timing failure, but would require separate approval and must not be relabelled actionable.

### SH10 — A correct payment formula is a baseline, not an advantage

Official allocation-round material distinguishes negative-price support rules; the AR4 Q&A says no difference payment during negative reference-price periods. LCCC's portal advertises historical eligible generation/payments from 2016, but the inspected entry does not establish individual project receipts and historical revisions. [Government scheme update](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/1040749/cfd-capacity-market-scheme-update-2021.pdf), [LCCC dataset catalog](https://dp.lowcarboncontracts.uk/dataset/?res_format=JSON&tags=CfD&tags=CfD+Payment).

**Competition is almost the proposed signal:** Modo's own documentation explicitly describes allocation-round-specific negative-price exclusions and reports foregone CfD revenue. Its modeled accuracy and client results were not verified. [Modo model description](https://docs.modoenergy.com/pages/oct-2026/uk-europe/gb/standalone-solar/). Original energy-economics research also studies CfD design and producer incentives. [Energy Economics paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eneco.2024.107674), [Energy Policy paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enpol.2024.113981); abstracts/excerpts only, not replications.

**Possible increment and counterargument:** an unforeseen negative-price episode could affect retained parent earnings differently after ownership, hedges, curtailment and route-to-market terms. Those economic shares and an independent asset-level receipt outcome were not established. A public aggregate portfolio payment cannot stand in for a named generator's cash.

**Cheapest falsification:** require one historical contract, same-asset generation/payment outcome and dated listed-parent interest; compare the interpretation with the provider's existing contract model. Reject if our contribution is only applying the published formula or if parent economics depend on private hedges. No contract/generation panel or negative-price series was acquired.

## Concrete verification proposal; not executed or approved

**Maximum two analyst-hours, eight public-document starts and 25 MiB, zero paid services/accounts/cloud jobs.** Confine it to the already identified Cox/Chevron measurement control. No paid PACER, national lease census, other debtor, bond-data purchase, price series or parser framework. The known later letters are controls; they cannot provide earlier predictor fields.

1. Locate the original December 12, 2023 motion and the relevant sale/abandonment attachment through legitimate free originals. Establish the document's public availability separately from its signature or docket date. If free access is unavailable, stop without rescue or account creation.
2. Select at most two lease IDs by ascending canonical ID from the attachment, before detailed matching. Verify the relevant accrued obligation and Chevron predecessor interest using dated assignment/operating records. A former-name match alone fails.
3. Determine whether same-lease engineering costs and applicable recoverable security were publicly measurable **then**. Preserve ranges and joint/several uncertainty.
