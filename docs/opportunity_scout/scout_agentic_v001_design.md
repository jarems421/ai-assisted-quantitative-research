# SCOUT_AGENTIC_V001_DIVERGER_CRITIC_DESIGN

**Documentation only; no runtime, tool, Skill, agents or calls activated.** Test whether a better single-Claude prompt improves Scout before considering this separate architecture. No N1/V004 permission, account, reservation or unused allowance transfers to it.

Proposed bounded shape: three independent ideators (relational analogy; inversion/qualified absence; obligations/flows) receive the same approved original input pool and no other generated cards. After their outputs are sealed, one optional recombiner may see the pool. Three independent critics inspect the same resulting pool: economic transmission/counterfactual; measurement/joins/clocks; duplicate/ordinary-explanation novelty. Critics do not see one another's assessments or negotiate consensus. One synthesizer may then see the candidates and sealed critiques and select final full-schema cards. A critic never supplies a human M/H/novelty judgment or verified source availability.

That is seven parent calls without the optional recombiner and eight with it, versus one in the proposed single-call baseline. All visible generations, context replay/cache/output, failed calls, coordination work, synthesis/formatting and human review must count. A synthesis or formatting call is a generation, not free local processing. Fixed per-call and aggregate budgets, maximum visible continuations, failure/stop rules, one-use journals and privacy scope need a new prospective approval; no open-ended debate, repair, reflection or best-of loop is implied.

Compare this against the best independently validated single-call prompt on matched eligible inputs and a fixed independent review procedure, reporting unique M-investigation-worthy mechanisms per human hour and per resource use separately. Context independence is procedural plus auditable input separation, not proof of independent model priors. More agents may add correlated errors, anchoring, inflated context cost and review burden. Agent-produced novelty/feasibility ratings are not ground truth.

An idea archive can support duplicate review only after independent generation, with human mechanism clustering; it must not leak one experimental arm's ideas into another's prompt. Graph2Idea is a relational-context research reference, not permission to change Graph R4 or treat graph edges as original evidence. No retrieval, source acquisition, returns, backtests or trades are part of this design.

These findings motivate a future test; they do not establish superiority for economic research. No third-party implementation was copied.
