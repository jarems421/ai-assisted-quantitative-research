1. WHY THE DIRECTION IS CHANGING
======================================================================

The three bounded pilots revealed a recurring sequencing problem.

The old sequence was approximately:

interesting mechanism
    ->
score candidate family
    ->
freeze source route
    ->
discover whether the source is actually accessible / outcome-complete

The new sequence should be:

authoritative source surface
    ->
prove access
    ->
prove denominator semantics
    ->
prove timing semantics
    ->
prove historical depth
    ->
prove entity/identity fields
    ->
estimate observation / clustering geometry
    ->
only then ask which economic mechanisms are testable from that surface

The principle is:

    PROVE THE RESEARCH SURFACE BEFORE SELECTING THE RESEARCH IDEA.

Do not interpret this as giving up on economic reasoning.

Economic mechanisms still matter.

They simply enter AFTER a source has demonstrated that an honest
historical experiment can plausibly be constructed from it.

6. SOURCE SURFACE, NOT HYPOTHESIS FAMILY
======================================================================

- a filing system;
- an official transaction register;
- an official regulatory-publication register;
- an official patent/event register;
- an official administrative dataset;
- another structured primary-source panel.

A source surface should be evaluated independently of whether its
events are bullish, bearish or profitable.

Examples of questions:

Can we enumerate all records of the relevant class?

Can we know what record existed at a historical time?

Can we distinguish updates/revisions?

Can we obtain stable identifiers?

Can we define the denominator without future outcomes?

Can we determine information timing?

Do records link to corporate entities?

Is there enough historical depth?

Does the surface generate many economically meaningful observations?
