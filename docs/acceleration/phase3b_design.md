# Phase 3B: revenue growth acceleration design

**DRAFT_NOT_REGISTERED_NOT_FROZEN. Design only, 2026-10-12.** Revenue growth acceleration is the sole proposed filing-derived family. No historical signal series, portfolio or performance result is constructed. [Source requirements](phase3b_source_requirements.json), [trial budget](phase3b_trial_budget.md) and [gate review](phase3b_gate_review.md) define the remaining conditions. EXP005 does not exist; EXP004 remains paused.

## Hypothesis and competing explanations

Newly disclosed improvement in consolidated annual revenue growth predicts positive subsequent net equity returns beyond a matched conventional momentum portfolio. Slow diffusion, underreaction, persistent demand and delayed repricing are plausible mechanisms, not findings. Annual reporting may add little because earnings releases and prices already incorporated the news. Revenue growth can also reflect acquisition rather than organic demand.

Momentum, size, sector, market beta and liquidity exposures; clustered filing dates; survivor selection; future restatements; accounting changes; corporate actions; search and trading costs can explain apparent improvement. The first experiment tests an incremental portfolio effect, not identified causal alpha or analyst earnings surprise. No historical expectations source is available. Lower drawdown alone would not support the return hypothesis.

## Annual revenue contract

For issuer fiscal year t, require **three consecutive original 10-K snapshots**, F(t-2), F(t-1), F(t). Each supplies its own current-year consolidated revenue R; prior snapshots stay pinned to their original accession, source SHA-256, element locators, parser/configuration/code hashes and eligibility evidence.

```text
g(t)   = R[F(t), t] / R[F(t-1), t-1] - 1
g(t-1) = R[F(t-1), t-1] / R[F(t-2), t-2] - 1
a(t)   = g(t) - g(t-1)
```

This deliberately uses three first-reported current-period observations. A current filing's comparative value is a **comparability check**, never a replacement denominator. Certify that each adjacent filing's comparative for the predecessor period agrees with that predecessor snapshot within the combined disclosed rounding intervals and that accounting basis and reporting entity are compatible. Intervals overlap only establishes numeric consistency; independent accounting-policy evidence is also required. An absent comparable, changed revenue recognition, material entity-scope change, discontinued-operation re-presentation, merger/spin-off or incompatible rounding/basis makes the chain unavailable. No synthetic pro-forma adjustment, currency conversion or organic-growth reconstruction is permitted. Ordinary business acquisitions need documented assessment; no assumption that unchanged concept means unchanged economic perimeter.

Acceleration is a dimensionless difference, not percentage growth of growth.

No secondary filing family is proposed in this budget. Operating cash flow could motivate a separately reviewed experiment, but can be negative and requires its own denominator semantics. EPS remains excluded because split/share-base comparative changes are unresolved; net-income and analyst-surprise features receive no slots. Rejecting revenue does not automatically authorize an alternative.

## Decisions, construction and matched controls

Annual original filing events update the signal only after contributor eligibility. At 21:00 New York on the calendar day before the first full exchange session each month, build the frozen as-of panel; execute at that session's 09:30 open. Only completed prior-session prices published by the decision cutoff enter. An eligibility open on execution day is too late for that prior-evening decision and waits until the next monthly decision. This explicit monthly quantization applies equally to momentum and fundamental ranks.

At each monthly decision use the newest eligible unaffected triple, no more than **400 calendar days since the newest original filing's eligibility**. Late/weekend/holiday disclosures follow the approved calendar policy; no intramonth event trade. Same-issuer repeated originals conflict and fail rather than double-count. If an amendment/non-reliance notice becomes eligible, invalidate affected state at the next scheduled decision. Missing or stale signals drop from the common panel, with coverage gates applied. Exit unselected positions at the next rebalance; held positions retain documented corporate-action treatment between rebalances. This is a one-month renewable holding rule, no alternate holding periods.

Rank acceleration descending; top **20** issuers, stable historical ID ascending tie; equal 95%/20 target weights, no leverage/short/derivatives/optimizer. Rebalance both sides against current weights, charging only actual turnover; holdings may differ but target cardinality, capital and exposure agree. N<100 fails participation; an otherwise insufficient K does not invoke a different sizing rule. Cash earns zero in all portfolios. At each window start fund independently, use only permissible pre-start source warm-up, no inherited P&L; mark end holdings at last close and disclose any forced terminal liquidation costs under the same convention.

| Control | Definition and fairness |
| --- | --- |
| Matched momentum — primary | Same full joint eligible panel, information cutoff, monthly dates, top20, 95% exposure, EW, funding, execution and costs. Rank preceding **252-to-21 completed-session total return**: product of (1+r) over sessions indexed j-251 through j-21 inclusive, where j is last completed session. Skip most recent 21 sessions. Includes cash dividends and dated split continuity once; no future corporate factors. |
| Eligible-universe EW | Same joint panel and timestamps, equal 95% exposure over all eligible issuers. Different cardinality is explicit; not the primary incremental comparator. |
| No-information ablation | Same top20/exposure and calendar; assign ranks with SHA-256 of fixed seed `3003001`, decision YYYYMMDD and stable ID, ascending. One deterministic allocation path; no seed search or shuffle-derived significance claim. |
| Market context | SPY buy-and-hold, if later sourced/certified, descriptive only. Not necessary for the primary gate and has no performance slot in this draft. |

The 12-minus-1 convention follows the prior 2–12 month momentum definition in [Kenneth French's construction notes](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/det_mom_factor.html), consulted for definition only, with a declared session-count approximation. This is not a reproduction of French's size-sorted long-short factor; no factor return files were accessed. No lookback search is allowed. Different names/trades can produce different realized costs despite identical cost rules; those differences are part of the estimand.

Report market beta, concentration, turnover and, only with certified dated sources, sector/size exposure differences descriptively. A sector/size matched strategy would require a new slot/design review; it is not an available rescue. An effect fully explained by measured exposures cannot be called independent alpha.

## Estimand, inference and advancement

Primary estimand: **252 × mean aligned daily net simple-return difference** d=r(revenue)-r(momentum), in percentage points/year. It is an arithmetic annualized mean difference, distinct from CAGR difference and standalone Sharpe. Include all scheduled cash days, dividends, terminal consideration, mark-to-market and actual trading costs. No pairwise deletion of unavailable return days; invalid/missing NAV invalidates the aligned comparison. Also report gross/net CAGR, sample volatility, zero-RF Sharpe, Sortino (zero target, existing convention), peak-to-trough drawdown, two-way traded notional/NAV turnover, costs and matched relative wealth. Reuse frozen metric definitions; no new metric to select a winner.

One candidate exists: no highest-Sharpe competition. Development advancement requires primary net difference at least **2 percentage points/year**, positive in at least four of six fixed calendar-year partitions, no coverage/identity/execution invalidation, positive under doubled costs and both declared timing delays. Validation requires the same 2-point mean floor and positive sign in at least two of three years plus all source/robustness gates. This is a directional interim filter, **not a significance claim**; no standalone three-year confirmation is made. Failure stops this family, not retuning on Validation.

Use whole-calendar synchronized paired blocks, preserving both complete portfolio paths and common-shock dates; never resample securities as independent observations. Primary descriptive noncircular moving-block bootstrap: **252 daily sessions/block, 10,000 draws, seed 3003001**, aligned complete daily net returns. Report 95% percentile intervals on mean d, annualizing endpoints by252. Existing `analysis.paired_bootstrap` provides descriptive mechanics; the EXP002 default20-session block is inappropriate for this planned annual persistence and is not inherited. Repeated issuers, overlapping renewable holdings and seasonal reporting motivate annual blocks. Dependence longer than one year and regime shifts remain limitations. Half-year126 and two-year504 block intervals are the two fixed sensitivity analyses; do not choose the narrowest interval. Joint-window descriptive resampling uses the same within-window stratification as the future null test; the existing single-window helper needs a separately verified extension before that use.

Formal confirmation is deferred to the **unchanged joint Validation+Test six-year path**, after owner-only Test authorization. Sample blocks within each funded window separately; never span its boundary. Minimum six nonoverlapping annual-block equivalents total (at least three/window), >=2,000 events and >=500 issuers/window, and complete aligned daily history are necessary, not evidence of six truly independent draws. Additionally require estimated effective daily sample size>=20 under both Bartlett HAC lag252 and504: N_eff=min(n,n×sample_variance(d)/long_run_variance(d)); compute lag products within funded windows only and normalize by total available observations. Nonpositive/nonfinite variance or inadequate lag pairs makes inference unavailable. This estimate is itself uncertain, not a power certificate; calibration must validate its use. If the fixed two-year interval includes zero, dependence invalidates annual blocks, or either effective-size floor fails, mark evidence insufficient. Only about three two-year-block equivalents are available, so low precision is expected. Shorter windows remain descriptive.

Predeclare a future, independently tested **null-centered** paired moving-block mean test: subtract the pooled observed mean from every daily d in both windows, preserving between-window mean contrasts; resample centered blocks within each window with original window lengths, calculate the pooled annualized mean; one-sided p=(1+number of bootstrap means >= observed mean)/(10001), H0 pooled mean<=0. The uncentered percentile distribution is not a null distribution. Pre-freeze synthetic size/calibration tests must assess the proposed procedure under overlapping holdings, annual clustering, persistent autocorrelation and window boundaries. If it lacks acceptable null calibration, this inferential contract needs review before returns, not a post-hoc replacement. Test must independently satisfy the same2-point mean floor,2/3 positive years and positive stressed effects as Validation; pooling cannot conceal a failed Test replication. No such test or return calculation is implemented in Phase3B.

Family-wise alpha0.05 with Holm across **four effects**: base, doubled cost, +1 execution session, +5 execution sessions. Failed/unresolved effects stay in family with p=1; controls and exposure summaries add no confirmatory claims. Reuse `analysis.holm` only on valid null-test p-values. Support requires positive primary and stressed effects, Holm-adjusted primary p<=0.05, primary 95% descriptive lower endpoint>0, 2-point primary economic floor, no critical falsifier and satisfactory fixed block sensitivities. Sensitivity intervals and year partitions are not additional optimized hypotheses. State approximation/low power and every family/trial searched; significance cannot remove observational confounding or coverage bias. Inferential implementation/calibration remains a **pre-freeze blocker**, so trial/inference design is PARTIAL.

## Costs, robustness and falsification

For every buy/sell charge **30 bps** of actual traded notional: commission/fees10, half-spread10, slippage/impact10. These are conservative planning allowances, not a measured vendor quote. Doubled-cost stress60bps applies to the complete same execution path; if changing cash/feasibility affects fills, re-simulate consistently rather than assert a fixed-fill deduction is a full execution test. Source-only liquidity/spread evidence and current vendor fee capability must justify or replace this model before results; any replacement requires a reviewed draft. Gross/net results and all-in costs are mandatory. Do not optimize a filter to lower measured costs. Require certified fill feasibility, daily open semantics, fractional-lot/reserve rules and corporate-action lifecycle at the fixed capital.

Two timing stresses shift **execution only** by +1 and +5 full sessions, keeping the original as-of panels/ranks and moving all portfolios equally; no new intervening information enters. They test delayed implementation, not proof of first-public availability. Other filing-delay policies, transforms, K, holding periods, momentum horizons and universes have no slots.

Three fixed contribution diagnostics per stage: remove the top single issuer's contribution, top five issuers combined, and top five positive daily contributions (top contributors chosen by contribution, reported transparently). Require primary incremental mean remains positive after each removal, and positive yearly signs as specified. These accounting decompositions cannot become substitute strategies; they count in the diagnostic budget.

Unsupported economic hypothesis: matched net effect below the materiality floor/nonpositive; costs or delays erase it; unstable yearly signs or issuer/date dominance; inadequate adjusted evidence. Design invalidation: survivor pruning, future comparative rewriting, ambiguous contexts, unapproved availability, unresolved identities, incomplete material source coverage, missing terminal values or population-changing execution. Neither category licenses a wider search. Report coverage-invalidated/inconclusive outcomes separately from economic falsification; stop or request a new pre-registered experiment, retaining all failures and historical trial counts.
