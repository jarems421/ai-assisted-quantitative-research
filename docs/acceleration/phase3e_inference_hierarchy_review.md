# Phase 3E-R inference hierarchy and materiality proposal

Frozen before v003 simulation; no real EXP005 inputs exist. One economic claim is proposed: the candidate has a positive net arithmetic paired mean relative to matched momentum on the same PIT population. Doubled cost and execution delays are mandatory stress/falsification conditions for that mechanism, not three additional discoveries. Formal one-sided family alpha remains 0.05 for the single primary endpoint. This withdraws the old promise of separately Holm-controlled stress significance; all previous four-effect protocols/results remain historical evidence. Owner review of the complete design is still required.

Phase 3B explicitly requires an **estimated** mean at least two annual percentage points, evidence against zero, and a positive lower endpoint of the primary 95% descriptive interval. Preserve that intent: estimated mean >=0.02 and positive two-sided 95% lower endpoint, plus the primary one-sided test and positive stressed estimates. A lower bound above 0.02 would be a stronger, different hypothesis. At the true estimated-floor boundary, full acceptance cannot have 80% power under a symmetric estimate; therefore report primary test power separately from complete materiality/stress-gate acceptance. The predeclared useful-power screen is 80% complete-support acceptance at a +5-point synthetic effect and 2.5-point annual tracking error, with 1/2.5/5/10-point noise sensitivity. This does not assert actual tracking error or plausible realized alpha.

Annual portfolio-level groups reflect reporting/holding persistence; the actual session counts provide weights so the estimator remains 252 times the mean daily difference. Independent funding is respected in the group partition. It does not create independent market shocks. Partial 2026 groups remain partial, not full years. Cross-year dependence, time-varying means and accounting selection are critical unresolved assumptions.

Two approaches are frozen: a session-weighted annual-group Student reference, and an exact independent symmetric-group sign test. The Student reference has an exact Gaussian model only when independent group-mean variances are inversely proportional to group session counts; short-memory session processes supply an approximate version that simulation must assess. The weighted kernel is **not** an implementation of the unweighted heterogeneous-group theorem in [Ibragimov–Müller](https://www.princeton.edu/~umueller/tstat.pdf). That paper motivates making group independence and approximate normality explicit, rather than claiming grouping creates robustness.

Sign inference targets a common additive location under independent symmetric group errors. It is not causal randomization. With only three/four/five groups, minimum one-sided p is 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32. Even five groups cannot provide a bounded two-sided 95% inversion: its minimum two-sided p is 1/16. Thus this second approach is a predeclared feasibility comparison with a known confidence-resolution failure, not a rescue method. Preserve its results and refuse its advancement regardless of apparent power.

Certification assumptions, stress worlds, seeds and acceptance gates are specified in the v003 protocol. Neither a synthetic pass nor policy approval certifies those assumptions for real portfolio returns. The stage comparison uses metadata/geometry and these synthetic results only. Real Development, Validation, Test and forward paper remain unopened.
