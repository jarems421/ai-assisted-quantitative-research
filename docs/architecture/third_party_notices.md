# Third-party integration provenance

Research OS adds clean-room compatible adapters and temporal semantics, with no copied upstream implementations or golden vectors. See [pinned registry](external_integrations.json) for upstream versions, licenses and inspected file hashes. Congressional schema/as-of semantics and chrono-ta duration/bucket concepts are credited to Austin Starks; their MIT implementations are not bundled. OpenBB provider-boundary concepts are credited to OpenBB (Apache2.0 at the audited commit). Nexus HTML projection calls the installed MIT-licensed nexustrade1.42.0 package offline; preserve that distribution's notices. NumPyBSD3 and Matplotlib's PSF-based notices remain in their installed distributions. No OpenBB/FinRL/pandas/sklearn/Rust/Node runtime or source copy is distributed by this change. External datasets require their own source/rights contract; software licensing does not certify primary evidence or availability.
